Plan collection of abandoned micromobility vehicles (Cascais)



Objetive: Create a tool to detect abandoned micromobility vehicles in real-time (defined as
vehicles parked outside a 30-meter radius of a station's coverage area for more than 120 minutes)
and calculate the optimal route for their collection.




Core Functionalities and Deliverables:
Real-Time Detection: Identify vehicles that remain abandoned outside the designated station
areas (30-meter buffer) for over two hours.
Dynamic Route Optimization: Calculate and continuously update the optimal collection route in
real-time as the pool of abandoned vehicles changes (whether due to new abandonments,
removals by the operator's maintenance team, or a user starting a new trip).
Supply/Demand KPIs: Define Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for supply and demand by
location, which can be applied to existing stations or other areas
